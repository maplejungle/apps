Lee's Lease Ledger

Type in each dealer's numbers as their emails come in. The ledger figures out what every offer really costs per month, checks the dealer's math, and lines the deals up side by side.

How to use this (four steps)
  1. Click "Start a new dealer." Fill in the dealer and the exact car — the ledger writes the quote-request email and starts a tracking card. Copy the email, send it from your own mail, then mark the card "Sent". If a dealer goes quiet for more than 5 days, the card will say so — silence is information too.
  2. When the reply arrives, click "Paste a dealer's reply" and paste the whole email — the ledger pulls out every number it can find, and you check them before anything is saved. If a dealer left things out, open their card and click "Draft a follow-up email" — it writes the request for exactly what's missing.
  3. Compare the big green number — "True monthly cost." It folds in the amount due at signing, the disposition fee, and any remaining VW payments you cover, so it's the one fair number to compare across dealers — even between a 36-month and a 39-month quote. Lowest wins.
  4. Picked a winner? Open its "Pre-signing checklist" and take it to the dealership — it lists the things that get agreed by email and then quietly differ on the contract.
