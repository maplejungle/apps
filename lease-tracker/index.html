Lee's Lease Ledger

Type in each dealer's numbers as their emails come in. The ledger figures out what every offer really costs per month, checks the dealer's math, and lines the deals up side by side.

How to use this (three steps)
  1. Send your email to each dealer. It asks for exactly the numbers this page needs.
  2. When a quote arrives, click "Paste a dealer's email" and paste the whole reply — the ledger pulls out every number it can find, and you check them before anything is saved. (You can also click "Add a deal" and type the numbers in yourself.) If a dealer left things out, open their card and click "Draft a follow-up email" — it writes the request for exactly what's missing.
  3. Compare the big green number — "True monthly cost." It folds in the amount due at signing, the disposition fee, and your remaining VW payments, so it's the one fair number to compare across dealers. Lowest wins.
